ITEM IDENTIFICATION(SKU MANAGEMENT) AND CORE STORE KEEPING

Contributor: Laurel

Feature 1- SKU Creation and Produt Identification

Description:
The system assigns a unique Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) to each product or product variant. It stores identifying information such as product name, category, brand, size, and other relevant details.

Purpose:
To uniquely identify products, prevent confusion between similar items, and make products easier to search, locate, and manage within the inventory.

Feature 2 — Stock-In and Stock-Out Management

Description:
The system records all inventory entering and leaving the store. When new goods are received, the stock quantity is increased, and when goods are issued or sold, the quantity is reduced.

Purpose:
To maintain an accurate record of available stock and provide a clear history of inventory movement.
